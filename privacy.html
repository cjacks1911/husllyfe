Privacy Policy

HUSLLYFE has no account, no sign-up, no server and no analytics. Nothing you enter is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no registration, no login, no email address and no identifier of any kind. HUSLLYFE never asks who you are, and has no way of finding out.

Where your data lives

Everything you record — training sessions, lifts, income and account balances, contributions, vehicles and their service history — is written to your own browser's local storage on the device you entered it on. It is never uploaded. There is no database, no cloud sync and no backup held by anyone but you.

This has a consequence worth stating plainly: browser storage is a cache your browser is entitled to clear, and clearing site data or reinstalling the app will delete your records permanently. HUSLLYFE offers a backup you can download and prompts you to take one. That file is the only copy, and it stays on your device unless you move it somewhere yourself.

The one request that leaves your device

The Garage can decode a VIN and check for open safety recalls. Those two lookups are sent to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, a United States government agency, at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov and api.nhtsa.gov. Only the VIN, or the year, make and model, is sent. Nothing else about you or your data is included, and the request is only made when you press the button that makes it.

These responses are deliberately never stored offline. A recall answer quietly served from an old cache would be worse than no answer, so with no signal the lookup simply does not return.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. No analytics script, no tag manager, no advertising network, no tracking pixel, no cookies set by this site and no third-party session recording. Photographs on the site are loaded from Unsplash, which like any image host receives the request that fetches them.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, rented, shared or disclosed, because it is never received in the first place. No third party has access to it. No law enforcement request could produce it, since there is nothing held to produce.

Children

HUSLLYFE is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. As it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, portability and deletion normally require asking an operator to act on data they hold. Here they are yours directly: every record is on your device and editable, the backup file is your export, and clearing site data or uninstalling the app is a complete and irreversible deletion that requires no one's cooperation.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date below. If a future version of HUSLLYFE ever transmits anything, that will be stated here explicitly and before it happens.
