The gauge should
reward consistency.

Transparent by design — every number below traces back to a session you logged or a slider you set. The goal you pick reweights them.

25%Training consistency
30%Strength progression
15%Recovery
10%Cardiovascular conditioning
20%Strength days
Adherence window = 28 days  |  Cardio load window = 14 days
Aerobic target = 150 min/wk  |  Strength target = 2 days/wk

CONSISTENCY   = days trained / (your days-per-week × 4) × 100
STRENGTH DAYS = days with a strength session / 8 × 100
CARDIO         = (cardio min × intensity / 2 weeks) / 150 × 100
STRENGTH PROG = computed on the Strength page —
                50 + (average gain over your starting maxes × 2.5)
RECOVERY       = manual — how recovered you actually feel

Consistency is graded against the commitment YOU set, not a fixed
seven-day week and not your own drifting average. Cardio and
Strength Days are the two halves of the WHO/CDC guideline and
count different things — minutes for one, days for the other — so
the same session cannot score twice.

All capped at 100. BODY SCORE = the five, weighted by goal.

An input with no data does not score zero and does not get a
default — its weight is redistributed across the inputs that do
have data, and the box above shows the weights actually in use.
The same is true one level up: the HUSLLYFE score starts from
BODY 40 / WEALTH 40 / DRIVE 20 and reweights across whichever
engines have something real to measure.
